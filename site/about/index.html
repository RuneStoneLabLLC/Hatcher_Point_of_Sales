About Hatcher Supply

A local supply store built on practical help.

Since 1992, Hatcher Supply has served local homeowners, farmers, gardeners, landscapers, pet owners, and bird lovers from its Huntingtown location.

Family owned

Rooted in Calvert County for more than three decades.

Hatcher Supply is the kind of store where useful advice matters as much as what is on the shelf. Customers come in for feed, seed, mulch, stone, pet food, propane, and everyday home essentials, and they can count on staff who understand the products and the local needs behind them.

The store's strength is simple: practical inventory, local knowledge, and a team willing to help load purchases into vehicles so customers can get back to the work waiting at home.

What customers can expect

Clear choices, useful products, and neighborly service.

Local Knowledge

Product guidance for Southern Maryland lawns, gardens, pets, birds, horses, and livestock.

Useful Inventory

Common farm, feed, landscape, pet, bird, and home supplies in one convenient local stop.

Hands-On Help

Loading support for many purchases, plus scheduled delivery for select bulk materials.
